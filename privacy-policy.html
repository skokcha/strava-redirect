Privacy Policy

MatchesLeft · Effective April 28, 2026

The short version: MatchesLeft does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers. We don't have servers. Everything stays on your device.

What We Collect

Nothing. MatchesLeft has no backend, no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. There is no way for us to receive your data because there is no place for it to go.

Where Your Data Lives

All ride records, training metrics, workout history, FTP and W Prime values, settings, and connected device information are stored only on your device, in the app's local database and storage.

If you uninstall MatchesLeft, that data is deleted with the app. We have no copies. We cannot recover it.

Bluetooth Devices

MatchesLeft connects to Bluetooth Low Energy fitness devices — power meters, heart rate straps, and smart trainers — to read your live ride data. These connections happen directly between the device and your phone. No data passes through our systems, because we don't have any.

Strava Integration (Optional)

If you choose to connect a Strava account, the app uses Strava's standard OAuth flow to obtain an access token from Strava. That token is stored only on your device — we never see your Strava username, password, or token.

When you tap "Upload to Strava" on a ride, the app sends that ride's GPX data directly from your device to Strava using the token you authorized. We are not involved in that transfer.

You can disconnect Strava at any time from the Settings screen, which deletes the locally stored token.

Permissions

The app requests these device permissions only for the purposes shown:

  • Bluetooth — to connect to your power meter, heart rate strap, and trainer.
  • Location — required by Android for any app that scans Bluetooth devices, and also used (only when you start a ride) to record GPS distance and elevation. On Android, background location access is used to continue GPS recording when the screen locks during an outdoor ride.

None of this information leaves your device.

No Ads, No Tracking, No Analytics

MatchesLeft contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting services, and no third-party trackers. The app does not phone home.

Subscription

MatchesLeft is available as a monthly subscription ($19.99/month) or an annual subscription ($149.99/year), with a 30-day free trial starting from your first ride. There is no account to create. You do not need to provide an email address or any personal information to use the app.

Children's Privacy

MatchesLeft is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no data from anyone, we collect no data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, an updated version will be published with a new effective date. Because we don't have your contact information, we cannot notify you directly — please check this page if you want to confirm the current terms.
